# About Me

I'm working as an Assistant Professor in the School of Electrical Engineering at the Manipal Institute of Technology (MIT), Bengaluru, India, from June 2026 to Till date.

Prior to this, I worked as an Assistant Professor (Senior Scale) at the School of Computer Science, University of Petroleum and Energy Studies (UPES), Dehradun, India, from December 2024 to June 2026.

Prior to this, I was a Post-Doctoral Fellow at the Learning and Extraction of Acoustic Patterns Lab (LEAP-Lab), headed by Prof. Sriram Ganapathy, Dept. of Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, India, from November 2023 to December 2024.

Prior to this, I worked as a Post-Doctoral Fellow at the Intelligent User Interface Lab (IUI-Lab), headed by Prof. Yukiko I Nakano, Dept. of Computer and Information Science, Seikei University, Tokyo, Japan, from May 2021 to March 2023.

I completed my Ph.D. under the supervision of Prof. Deepu Vijayasenan at the Electronics and Communication Engineering department, from National Institute of Technology Karnataka - Surathkal, Mangalore, India, in June 2021.

Prior to that, I did my Master of Technology in Digital Electronics and Communication System (2011-2013), and Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and Communication Engineering (2007-2011) from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Anantapur (JNTUA), Andhra Pradesh, India.
